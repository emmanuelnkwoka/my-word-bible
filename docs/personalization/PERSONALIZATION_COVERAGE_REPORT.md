## 4. Why most verses are not personalised
The largest exclusion reasons (KJV) are: words to characters in narrative; addressed to God; first-person voice; judgement or warning context; and the dialogue of Job. Most "flagged" verses are divine speech to a people (the prophets), covenant address in the Torah, letters to named individuals, and verses whose addressee is unclear. Per-book counts are in the workbook (sheet 8) and on the Review Centre's book list.

## 5. What this means in the app today
- **Normal builds:** with 0 approvals, every passage shows in **Mode C** (original Scripture; the Daily Word is titled "Daily Word – MWB").
- **Reviewer builds** (the APK installed on your phone) with the review preview on: proposals that pass validation appear in Daily Word, on Home and in sharing, **labelled "Review preview · not yet approved"**. On your phone, today's Daily Word (Romans 15:13) shows Mode B: "Emmanuel Nkwoka, abound in hope" above the unchanged KJV text.
- Once a named reviewer approves a proposal (in the app or through the import tool), it appears in Mode A or B without the preview label.
