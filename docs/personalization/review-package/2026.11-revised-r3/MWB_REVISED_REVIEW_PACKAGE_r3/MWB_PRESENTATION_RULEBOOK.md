# MY WORD BIBLE — Presentation Rulebook
Package 2026.11-revised-r2 · 2026-11-13 · Reviewer: MY WORD BIBLE — Internal Theological and Linguistic Review

## 0. The one governing question
For every verse: **"Given what this Scripture actually says, who spoke it, whom it addressed, and what it means in context, what is the most faithful and meaningful way this believer can personally receive it?"**

No verse is abandoned. No verse is rewritten to pretend it was addressed only to the reader. Every verse has a final, documented presentation.

## 1. The three things that must never be confused
- **A. Canonical Scripture** — the exact KJV/WEB text. Never silently altered.
- **B. Personalised Scripture rendering** — a controlled name-in-text (Mode A) or name-in-title (Mode B) presentation that passes all integrity checks.
- **C. Editorial personal reception** — a separate explanation/application/prayer (Mode C/D). **Never** shown as canonical wording, never attributed to God/Jesus/an author as the biblical text.

## 2. Disposition
`approvedPresentation` = this presentation passed the **internal product review**. It does **not** mean an altered rendering became canonical Scripture, and it does **not** record the approval of the designated supervisor (Pastor Obi Ogbo), who is never impersonated. Human ratification remains a separate, real step.

## 3. The six presentation types
| Type | What the reader sees | Canonical text | When chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| `directPersonalisation` | Verse with the name as a comma-set vocative | unchanged (only a vocative added) | Representative address where grammar/speaker/audience permit (e.g. Proverbs learner, third-person-God Psalm). |
| `personalTitle` | A faithful title with the name **above** the unchanged verse | unchanged | Collective encouragement/teaching where a modest faithful title reflects the verse. |
| `personalReception` | The unchanged verse **plus** a separate editorial reception line | unchanged + editorial | Divine speech, covenant promise, collective "we/us", Christ's words, named letters — received in Christ without rewriting. |
| `personalReflection` | The unchanged verse + a reflective application/question | unchanged + editorial | Where a reception as statement is less fitting than a reflection. |
| `prayerReception` | The unchanged verse + a first-person prayer drawn from it | unchanged + editorial | Verses addressed to God / first-person voice (the reader prays them). |
| `originalScriptureOnly` | The verse exactly as written | unchanged | The normal reading experience — and the honest default for the great majority of Scripture, including narrative, law, genealogy, warning and judgement. |

## 4. The non-negotiable boundaries (enforced on every record)
1. **Speaker integrity** — God stays God; Jesus stays Jesus; Paul/psalmist/prophet stay themselves.
2. **Christology** — Christ's "I am" self-declarations are never transferred to the reader (John 11:25: the reader believes, he does not become the resurrection).
3. **Audience** — Israel, the church, Timothy, Gaius are preserved; the reader receives *through Christ*, never by erasing the first audience (Isaiah 43:1, 41:10).
4. **Promise integrity** — collective/conditional promises are never turned into unconditional personal guarantees (3 John 1:2 is an apostle's affectionate wish, not a health-wealth promise).
5. **Warnings stay warnings** — judgement, woe and rebuke keep their genuine meaning; they are never reframed as comfort.
6. **No fabrication** — no invented promise, divine statement, or biblical quotation; where nothing faithful is possible, the original stands (`originalScriptureOnly`).

## 5. How Mode C/D receptions are written
Modern, natural English, clearly editorial, name resolved dynamically (full / first / preferred / none — never hard-coded). A reception names the true speaker and audience, keeps conditions and collective dimensions, distinguishes historical context from present-day reception, and never claims to be the words of God/Jesus/an author. The curated reception set in this package is faithful and verified; it is a **growing** layer — new receptions are added the same way, not mass-generated.

## 6. Worked governing examples (the standard for the whole corpus)
- **Isaiah 43:1 / 41:10** (divine speech to Israel): canonical preserved; `personalReception` received in Christ; the name is **not** inserted into God's covenant speech.
- **Ephesians 2:10 / Romans 8:37** (collective "we"): `personalReception` in second person; the canonical "we/us" is not rewritten.
- **John 11:25** (Christ's self-declaration): `personalReception`; "I am the resurrection" stays His.
- **Romans 15:13** (Paul's benediction): `personalTitle` "Emmanuel, abound in hope" + `personalReception` benediction; original unchanged.
- **3 John 1:2** (John's wish to Gaius): `personalReception` as affectionate apostolic wish, soul-and-body flourishing in parallel — never a divine guarantee.
- **Proverbs 1:8 / 23:18** (father-to-son wisdom): `personalReception` carrying the enduring wisdom and its condition, without inventing a direct parental word from God.
