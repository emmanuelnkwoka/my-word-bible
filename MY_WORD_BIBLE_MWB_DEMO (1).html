The same date does not have to mean the same experience. An empty signal yields the scheduled word; a live signal yields the most relevant candidate. Nothing is tracked — this panel simulates signals locally.

How Phase 7B scoring works. Category match +50 · life-situation match +40 · recently-shown candidates excluded (freshness) · repeating yesterday's category −15 (diversity) · ties break deterministically by candidate code. No signal, or no match, falls back to the scheduled base word. Feb 29 always yields the reserved leap candidate (Revelation 21:5).
